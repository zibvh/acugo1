BIXCART SAFETY

Safety tips

  • Keep important conversations and transaction details on Bixcart.
  • Never share your password, card PIN, OTP, or authentication codes.
  • Check the item and seller details before completing a purchase.
  • Do not send money outside the Bixcart payment flow for a Bixcart order.
  • Meet in safe, appropriate campus locations when an in-person handoff is agreed.
  • Report suspicious listings, messages, or users to an admin.
