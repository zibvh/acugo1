About Bixcart

Bixcart is a student marketplace built to make buying and selling within the campus community simpler, safer, and more accountable.

Buyers can discover products, pay through Bixcart, and use the platform's escrow workflow. Sellers can list products for free and receive their payout after the order completes and escrow is released.

Bixcart currently serves Ajayi Crowther University and is designed to grow with the student community.
